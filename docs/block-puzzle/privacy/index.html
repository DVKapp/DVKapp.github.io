Block Puzzle 8x8 Privacy Policy

Clear, transparent privacy principles for Block Puzzle 8x8: Neon Master. No accounts, no personal tracking, and local progress storage.

Developer brand: DVKapp. Support: game@dvk.vn.

Your Privacy

Block Puzzle 8x8: Neon Master is developed under DVKapp. Contact: game@dvk.vn. Updated 18 October 2026. No account registration, real name, phone number, or precise location is collected or required.

On-Device Local Progress & Storage

Your high scores, Story Saga stars, player levels, 21-day quest streak, coins, and booster inventory are stored locally and securely on your device. Uninstalling the app or resetting app storage will clear your local progress.

In-App Purchases (IAP)

Optional coin bundles and permanent No-Ads packages are securely authenticated and processed exclusively by Apple App Store and Google Play. DVKapp never accesses, processes, or stores payment card credentials.

Kid-Friendly Google AdMob Integration

The game utilizes Google Mobile Ads SDK for standard test/production banners placed safely under the logo to avoid accidental taps, as well as voluntary rewarded video ads. Players are never forced to watch ads during active puzzle gameplay.

Firebase Analytics & Diagnostics

Anonymous crash diagnostics and aggregated gameplay progression metrics are monitored via Firebase to ensure frame rate stability and eliminate bugs. All data handling complies with Google policies: policies.google.com/privacy.

Your Data Control Rights

You maintain complete control over your data. You may reset local progress directly in the Settings menu at any time. For support or privacy questions, contact game@dvk.vn.

About this website

This website has no account, contact form, advertising SDK, analytics script or tracking cookie. Email opens your own mail app. Hosted on GitHub Pages and managed through Cloudflare.
