Game Rules & Mechanics

01 • Classic 8x8 Grid Mechanics

Drag and drop vibrant candy-neon shapes from the tray onto the 8 × 8 grid. Complete full horizontal rows or vertical columns to blast blocks away and create room for upcoming pieces.

02 • Blazing Combo Streaks & Point Multipliers

Clear multiple rows or columns simultaneously or chain clears on consecutive moves to trigger high-octane multipliers (Combo x2, x3... up to x10+) paired with electrifying voice cheering.

03 • 8 Dynamic Game Modes

Explore 8 exciting modes: Classic Endless (pure relaxing puzzle), Story Saga (20 adventurous stages unlocking Galaxy Theme), Online Live Events (limited-time trophy tournament), Monthly Challenge (31-day trophy cup), Daily Quests & 21-Day Streak, Global Leaderboard, Visual Themes Shop, and Interactive Tutorial.

04 • Tactical Boosters & Next Queue Preview

Master tricky situations with power-up tools: Undo (revert your last placement), Rotate (change orientation of tray blocks), Hammer 3x3 (blast clear a 3 × 3 room), and Next Queue Preview to foresee 3 upcoming shapes.

05 • ADA Colorblind Accessibility & Pure Offline Play

Full ADA colorblind mode with geometric shape icons, tactile haptic feedback, and complete 100% offline play without requiring Wi-Fi or cellular data.

Coins, stars and trophies are in-game records and cannot be exchanged for cash.
