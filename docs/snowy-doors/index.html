Snowy Doors: Advent Calendar

An Advent calendar for the whole family! Every day your child tears the gift paper to open a door on a snowy street, plays a mini-game with Pip the baby reindeer and earns a decoration for their own snowy village. Missed doors wait for you, and the street glows brighter all the way to Christmas Eve.

Features

🎁 25 doors, 25 different games

Snowball toss, cookie icing, ornament catch, find Pip, present stacking, lantern memory, penguin counting and more, each door with its own way to play.

🦌 Pip is always there

Pip explains every game in a cheerful voice, cheers every win and helps after a few tries: the game gets a step easier and shows a hint.

🏘️ Your own snowy village

Every door gives a decoration to drag onto your street, and the string lights glow brighter with every door you open.

📅 Follows the real calendar

Holiday Countdown in November, Advent from December 1 to 25. Missed doors stay open to catch up.

✨ Decor Shop

Optional decoration packs for the sky, rooftops, street lamps and snowy ground, each with one free decoration to try.

👨‍👩‍👧 Safe for kids

No accounts, no chat, no analytics and no interrupting ads. A parental gate guards purchases, links and reminders. Works offline.

Optional purchases: four decoration packs (Magic Sky, Twinkly Rooftops, Street Lamps, Snowy Village) and the Everything Pack, each bought once and kept. All 25 doors and door gifts are free. All purchases and outside links are behind a parental gate.
