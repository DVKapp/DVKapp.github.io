Snowy Doors with Pip

Pip reads every game aloud

A snowy street with 25 wrapped doors, one for each day from December 1 to Christmas. Behind every door waits a different little game, and Pip the baby reindeer reads every rule out loud, so children who can't read yet can play on their own.

How to play

Four steps, about two minutes a day.

Name your street

On the first visit Pip says hello and your child picks a sign: Lollipop Lane, Pine Tree Lane, Twinkle Star Lane or Snowman Lane. No reading needed, just tap a picture.

Tear today's door

Every day one house on the street has a wrapped door. Swipe a finger across the paper to tear it open.

Play the game with Pip

Behind the door is a short mini-game. Pip reads the rules out loud, cheers when you win and comes to help when a game feels tricky.

Decorate your street

Every win gives a decoration (a snowman, a lantern, a cozy little fox and more). Drag it anywhere on your street and watch it glow brighter each day.

The 25 doors

December 1 to 25. On Christmas Day Pip flies the sleigh through the star rings and lights up every house.

For grown-ups

  • Frosty lock: purchases, outside links, reminders, the bonus video and "Open every door now" all ask a grown-up to tap a date written out in words on a December calendar.
  • Pip's notice board: sounds, music and Pip's voice switches, Pip's door bell reminder (never between 9 pm and 9 am) and "Open every door now" for families who start late.
  • Ads: no banner ads and no ads between games. Once a day a grown-up can choose to watch a short, child-directed video for one extra decoration.
  • Purchases: all 25 doors are free. Optional one-time decoration packs: Magic Sky, Twinkly Rooftops, Street Lamps, Snowy Village, or the Everything Pack.
  • Privacy: no accounts, no chat, no ad tracking. Progress stays on the device; anonymous usage statistics and crash reports help us fix problems. Read the privacy policy.

Questions

Does my child need to read?

No. Pip reads every instruction aloud, buttons use pictures, and the street name is chosen by tapping a sign.

We missed a day. Is the door gone?

No. Earlier doors stay open, so you can catch up any time. A game never ends just because the clock ran out.

Can we play before December?

Yes. In November a 30-door Holiday Countdown street opens; the first 24 doors each hide a different game. A grown-up can also switch on "Open every door now".

What age is it for?

Children about 4 to 8, ideally playing with a parent nearby. Games get a little harder through the month, and Pip helps after a loss.
