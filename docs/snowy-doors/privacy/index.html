Privacy at Snowy Doors with Pip

Answers for parents: what stays on the device, what anonymous statistics leave it, and how the Frosty lock keeps purchases and links for grown-ups.

WinPlay Studio · DVK · game@dvk.vn

What is Snowy Doors with Pip, and who makes it?

Snowy Doors with Pip is an Advent calendar game for families with young children, made by WinPlay Studio (DVK). App IDs: com.winplaystudio.snowyDoors (iPhone, iPad) and com.winplaystudio.snowy_doors (Android). Questions about this policy go to game@dvk.vn. The dvk.vn website has its own privacy page.

What stays on your device?

There is no account and no sign-in. The street name your child picks, the doors opened, stars, gifts, the decorations placed on the street, the "lamps lit in a row" count and every switch on Pip's notice board are saved only on the device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. We cannot see this information.

What do we never ask for?

We never ask for a name, email address, photo, contact list, voice recording or precise location. The game does not use the camera or microphone. There is no chat, no leaderboard, no messages between players and nothing that children can post. Pip's voice lines are recorded in advance and built into the app.

What leaves the device, and why?

Three things, all without names or accounts: - Anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase: for example "door 4 opened", "a game was won with 2 stars" or "the shop was opened". We use them to find doors that are too hard and to improve the game. Analytics is set to the child-safe mode: no advertising ID, no ad personalization and no ad-related consent. - Crash reports through Firebase Crashlytics: technical details about the device model, system version and what the app was doing when it crashed, so we can fix bugs. - The optional reward video (see the next answer). Firebase and Google may also process the device's IP address to deliver these services, as described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy and policies.google.com/privacy. We do not sell data and we do not use it to track anyone across other apps or websites.

Are there ads?

No banner ads and no ads between games. Once a day a grown-up can choose, after the Frosty lock, to play a short video from Google AdMob for one extra decoration. Ad requests are marked as child-directed and non-personalized, the game does not read the advertising ID and never asks for tracking permission. Google's ad policies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

How do purchases work?

All 25 doors, games and door gifts are free. The optional decoration packs (Magic Sky, Twinkly Rooftops, Street Lamps, Snowy Village and the Everything Pack) are sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play behind the Frosty lock. We never see your payment details; your store account and its family controls (Ask to Buy, Family Link) still apply.

What about reminders?

Pip's door bell is off until a grown-up switches it on behind the Frosty lock. The reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device, never sent between 9 pm and 9 am, and we run no push server. Switch them off any time on Pip's notice board or in the device settings.

What is the Frosty lock?

Before purchases, outside links, reminders, the bonus video and "Open every door now", the game asks a grown-up to tap a date written out in words on a December calendar. It protects children from leaving the game or spending money by accident.

Children's privacy laws

Snowy Doors is designed for children and families. We follow the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR rules for children, the Google Play Families policy and the Apple App Store guidelines for apps used by children.

How can parents delete data or ask questions?

Uninstall the app to delete everything stored on the device. To ask about or delete analytics and crash data linked to your device, write to game@dvk.vn with the approximate install date; we reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Snowy Doors handles data, we update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app.
