Snowy Doors with Pip: house rules

How the doors open, what gifts and decoration packs are, and how to play fair on your snowy street.

WinPlay Studio · DVK · game@dvk.vn

The short version

Snowy Doors with Pip is a family game by WinPlay Studio (DVK). Play it for fun at home, keep it fair, and grown-ups decide about purchases. The longer rules are below; the terms of the Apple App Store or Google Play also apply.

How the doors open

One door opens per day, following the device date: a 30-door Holiday Countdown in November and the Advent street from December 1 to 25. Missed doors stay open. A grown-up can switch on "Open every door now" on Pip's notice board. From January to October the last season's doors are all open.

Gifts and decorations

Stars, door gifts, the street name and decorations are virtual items with no money value. They cannot be sold, swapped or moved to another player, and they disappear if the app is deleted.

Decoration packs

Magic Sky (snowy.sky), Twinkly Rooftops (snowy.roof), Street Lamps (snowy.lights), Snowy Village (snowy.ground) and the Everything Pack (snowy.all) are optional one-time purchases that stay yours in future seasons. Each pack shows one free decoration first. Use "Restore purchases" in the Decor Shop on a new device. Prices appear in your local currency in the store, and refunds follow Apple's or Google's rules.

Please play fair

Do not modify, hack or reverse engineer the game. We may adjust games, gifts, decorations and prices to keep the game fun and balanced.

Who owns what

Pip, the 25 games, the art, music and voice lines belong to WinPlay Studio (DVK) and its licensors. You get a personal, non-transferable license to play on your own devices. Fonts: Mountains of Christmas (Apache License 2.0) and Baloo 2 (SIL Open Font License).

If something goes wrong

We work hard to keep the doors opening smoothly but cannot promise the game will always be free of errors, and features may change over time. Tell us at game@dvk.vn and we will help.
