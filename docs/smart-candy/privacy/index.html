Smart Candy: Match 3 Privacy Policy

Official privacy policy for Smart Candy: Match 3 (com.smartcandy.match3). Clear transparency on local saves, in-app purchases, and Google AdMob integration.

WIN Entertainments · DVK

Privacy · Terms

1. Who operates the game

Smart Candy - Match 3 (Android package com.smartcandy.match3) is provided by WIN Entertainments / DVK. This policy applies to the Android game, including its advertising, purchases and Google services. Updated: 23 October 2026. Contact: game@dvk.vn or kietdovanapp@gmail.com. The official policy is maintained at https://dvk.vn/smart-candy/privacy/.

2. Progress saved on your device

You do not need to create a player account to play. Levels, stars, scores, coins, lives, boosters and preferences are saved on your device. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can remove this progress. Android device backup may also apply according to your device settings; we do not promise cloud recovery of game progress. Advertising, purchases and Google services use an internet connection even though the puzzle gameplay can work offline.

3. Advertising and information processed by Google

The game includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). The SDK may collect and share your IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), advertising or other device identifiers, app/ad interactions, and diagnostic information such as performance and error reports. Google uses this information to serve and measure ads, provide analytics, and prevent fraud. These identifiers and technical records are not necessarily anonymous. We do not request access to precise device location, contacts, photos or your microphone for gameplay. Google explains its processing at policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Consent and advertising choices

Where required, Google User Messaging Platform presents a consent message with choices to consent, decline or manage options. In the updated Android game, open Privacy & Terms and select Ad privacy choices when that option is available to review the applicable consent form. You can continue puzzle gameplay if an ad is unavailable or you decline optional consent. Android settings also let you manage or delete your advertising ID and control app permissions. Changing an ad choice does not itself delete information already processed by Google.

5. Firebase and notifications

The Android app includes Firebase Analytics and Firebase Cloud Messaging. Google may process installation identifiers, app instance identifiers, device/app details, usage events and diagnostics for analytics and service operation. Messaging uses an installation identifier and registration token to route notifications. You can control notification permission in Android settings; disabling notifications does not by itself delete analytics or installation records. For service details and retention, see https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

6. Purchases and support

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. The app receives purchase status, product information and purchase tokens or receipts needed to deliver or restore supported purchases. We do not receive your full payment card or bank credentials. Google processes payment information under its own policies. If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any diagnostic details you choose to provide. Do not send passwords or payment card details.

7. Retention, deletion and your rights

Local saves remain until removed from your device, subject to device backup settings. Google services retain data according to their service settings and privacy policies; clearing game storage does not guarantee deletion of records already held by those services. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or object to certain processing by contacting game@dvk.vn. Describe your request and the app name; we may need proportionate information to identify records. We cannot identify every advertising record from a player name because the game has no player account. Support messages are retained only as needed to handle the request and applicable recordkeeping obligations.

8. Children, security and updates

The game contains advertising and optional purchases. Parents and guardians should supervise use, purchases and Android privacy settings. We do not ask children to enter a name or contact information through gameplay. Contact game@dvk.vn if you believe a child has provided personal information and request appropriate action. Data sent by the Google SDKs uses encrypted transport; no system can guarantee absolute security. Providers may process information in other countries under their published safeguards. Changes to this policy will appear on this page with an updated date; material changes will be communicated as appropriate.
