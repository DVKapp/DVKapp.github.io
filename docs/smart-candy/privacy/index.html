Smart Candy: Match 3 Privacy Policy

Official privacy policy for Smart Candy: Match 3 (com.smartcandy.match3). Clear transparency on local saves, in-app purchases, and Google AdMob integration.

WIN Entertainments · DVK

1. Overview & Developer Information

Smart Candy - Match 3 (Package: com.smartcandy.match3) is a casual puzzle game developed and published by WIN Entertainments and distributed under DVK (https://dvk.vn). Developer Contact: kietdovanapp@gmail.com / game@dvk.vn. Effective Date: October 20, 2026. We are deeply committed to safeguarding your privacy. Smart Candy - Match 3 is designed as an offline-first casual game. No account registration is required to enjoy the full game experience, and we do not collect personal identifiers such as your real name, email address, physical address, contact list, or biometric data.

2. Offline-First Architecture & On-Device Storage

Your gameplay data—including completed levels, star achievements, high scores, coin balances, lives, booster items, and audio preferences—is stored locally and securely on your device. Zero cloud registration or external server synchronization is needed. Because your save data is hosted entirely on your physical hardware, uninstalling the application or clearing application storage in Android system settings will permanently reset your local gameplay progress.

3. Google Play In-App Purchases (IAP)

Smart Candy - Match 3 offers optional in-app purchases for coin packs, extra moves, lives, and tactical booster items. All monetary transactions are processed, authorized, and secured exclusively by the Google Play Store via Google Play Billing Library (Version 8+). WIN Entertainments and DVK never access, capture, or store your credit card, debit card, bank credentials, or physical billing details. All financial purchases are governed strictly by the Google Play Terms of Service.

4. Advertising & Device Identifiers (Google AdMob & UMP)

To support ongoing maintenance and keep Smart Candy - Match 3 free to play, the game integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob, Publisher: pub-3636940103463538). In full accordance with our Google Play Data Safety declaration, Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google Play Services may collect and transmit: (a) Device or other IDs (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID); (b) Non-identifiable diagnostic metrics (ad impressions, performance, interaction events). All data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS protocols. We integrate the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent framework. Players in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and jurisdictions with privacy statutes can review, customize, or revoke consent for personalized advertising cookies and device identifiers at any time. You can also reset or delete your Google Advertising ID via Android Settings > Google > Ads.

5. Performance Analytics & Diagnostics

We utilize Firebase Analytics and Google Play Core telemetry to collect anonymous, aggregated technical metrics (such as framerate consistency, level pass rates, and crash stack traces). This data is used solely to resolve stability bugs, optimize game performance across various Android hardware configurations, and balance game stage difficulties.

6. Family Friendly & Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Smart Candy - Match 3 is crafted as an accessible, family-friendly puzzle experience suitable for audiences of all ages. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and Google Play Families Policy requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in applicable European territories). Where required by law or platform policies, advertising requests are flagged for child-directed treatment (TFCD/TFUA), serving strictly contextual, non-targeted family-appropriate advertisements.

7. Data Retention & Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Because we maintain no remote user databases or account systems, you retain autonomous control over your game data directly on your device. You may erase all locally stored data at any time via Android Settings > Apps > Smart Candy - Match 3 > Storage > Clear Data. Under GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and relevant privacy legislation, you have the right to inquire about data processing practices or withdraw ad consent. For inquiries, contact our Data Protection desk at kietdovanapp@gmail.com or game@dvk.vn.
