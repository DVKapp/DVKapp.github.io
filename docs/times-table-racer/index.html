Times Table Racer: Math Kids

Times tables as a real race! A sum lights up on the board, answer gates rush toward you, and you steer your toy race car into the right lane: tap left or right to change lanes, double-tap to zoom through. Pick the wrong gate and your car spins out while Rocco the raccoon shows the fact with dots. No game over, ever.

Features

🏁 1,080 races, 9 worlds

From first-grade adding, doubles and skip counting to tables 1–9, division, missing numbers and big tables up to 20 × 20. Every 12th race is a boss duel.

🧠 Tricky answer gates

Wrong gates are the mistakes kids really make: swapped digits, next-door numbers, a row off in the table. Facts you miss or answer slowly come back a few gates later.

🚗 54 cars to collect

Win a race to open a mystery capsule. Capsules are earned by playing only, never sold.

🔧 Upgrade your racer

Every car has speed, turbo, grip and brain skills. Spend the bolts you earn on upgrades, paint and decals.

📅 Daily Grand Prix

One shared race a day: race your own ghost from yesterday and keep your streak.

✏️ Practice lane

Practice any table from 1 to 20 with dot pictures and Rocco counting along.

📊 Parent Report

A heat map of every fact up to 20 × 20 and what to practice next (optional purchase).

👨‍👩‍👧 Safe for kids

No accounts, no chat, child-directed ads with no tracking, and a parental gate for purchases, links and sharing. Works offline.

Optional purchases: No Ads, bolt packs, Nitro packs and the Parent Report. All purchases, outside links and sharing are behind a parental gate.
