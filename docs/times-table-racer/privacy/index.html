Times Table Racer: Math Kids Privacy Policy

How Times Table Racer handles data: no accounts, progress kept on your device, child-directed non-personalized ads and a parental gate.

WinPlay Studio · DVK · game@dvk.vn

1. Who we are

Times Table Racer: Math Kids (iOS/iPadOS: com.winplaystudio.timesTableRacer, Android: com.winplaystudio.times_table_racer) is a math racing game made by WinPlay Studio (DVK) for iPhone, iPad and Android. Questions about this policy: game@dvk.vn. This policy applies to the game; the dvk.vn website has its own privacy page.

2. No accounts, progress stays on your device

The game has no account and no sign-in. Your races, places, stars, bolts, Nitro, cars, capsules, skills, streaks, practice results, the Parent Report and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data. The only data that leaves your device is described below (ads and in-app purchases).

3. Made for children

Times Table Racer is designed for children in grades 1 to 5 and their families. We do not ask for or collect real names, email addresses, photos, contacts, voice recordings or location. There is no leaderboard, no chat, no player-to-player contact and no user-generated content. Rival cars in races are computer players. All purchases, links to outside websites and sharing are behind a parental gate. We follow the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Google Play Families policy and Apple's App Store guidelines.

4. No analytics or crash reports

The game does not send analytics or crash reports, and we do not collect any analytics or crash data about you.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Ad requests are configured as child-directed and non-personalized, and the game does not use the advertising ID or ask for tracking permission. Google may process device information and IP address to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's policies at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy. Watching an optional reward video is always a choice. The No Ads purchase removes banner and between-race ads.

6. Notifications

The game can send optional reminders (a new daily race, keeping your streak). They are local notifications scheduled on the device itself and are only sent after the player or a parent turns them on. No reminders are sent between 9 pm and 9 am. We do not use a push notification server. You can turn them off in the game or in your device settings at any time.

7. In-app purchases

Optional purchases (No Ads, bolt packs, Nitro packs and the Parent Report) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Mystery capsules can only be earned by playing and are never sold. Your store account and its parental controls (Ask to Buy, Family Link) also apply.

8. Sharing

The Daily Grand Prix result can be shared as a short text with the race time and a row of squares. Sharing is behind a parental gate, uses your device's share sheet, and we do not receive what you share.

9. Your choices and data deletion

You can remove all game data by uninstalling the app. Parents can contact game@dvk.vn with any question; we will answer within 30 days.

10. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Contact: game@dvk.vn.
