Doodle Arrows

Draw the missing arrows. Every page of the sketchbook is missing a few arrows, and you draw them with your finger. On-device AI reads your doodle, then you launch every arrow off the paper.

How to play

  • Draw an arrow in each empty square.
  • Tap Check. If an arrow would get stuck, Scribbs the pencil shows you which one. No penalty, just redraw.
  • Launch the arrows. They only fly if their path is clear.
  • From level 16, Wiggle Arrows crawl along a path you draw.

For iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch

  • 300 levels across 20 sketchbook pages, plus a Daily Mini every day.
  • Doodle Lab: draw your own puzzle and share it as a short code.
  • Play the Daily Mini on a Home Screen widget, follow your level in a Live Activity, ask Siri for your streak.

For parents

Made for children aged 6 to 8. No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no tracking. Works offline; drawings never leave the device. Links, sharing and resetting progress are behind a grown-up gate.
