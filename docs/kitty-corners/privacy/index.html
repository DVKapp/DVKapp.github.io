Kitty Corners: Cat Puzzle Privacy Policy

How Kitty Corners handles data: no accounts, progress kept on your device, child-directed ads, an optional anonymous leaderboard and a parental gate.

WinPlay Studio · DVK · game@dvk.vn

1. Who we are

Kitty Corners: Cat Puzzle (iOS/iPadOS: com.winplaystudio.kittyCorners, Android: com.winplaystudio.kitty_corners) is a cat logic puzzle game made by WinPlay Studio (DVK) for iPhone, iPad and Android. Questions about this policy: game@dvk.vn. This policy applies to the game; the dvk.vn website has its own privacy page.

2. No accounts, progress stays on your device

The game has no account and no sign-up. Your levels, coins, hints, trophies, streaks, cat outfits and settings are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data. The only data that leaves your device is described below (ads, Firebase and the optional leaderboard).

3. Made for all ages, including young children

Kitty Corners is designed for all ages, including young children. We do not ask for or collect real names, email addresses, photos, contacts, voice recordings or precise location. There is no chat and no free-text input. All purchases, links to outside websites and joining the leaderboard are behind a parental gate. We follow the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Google Play Families policy and Apple's App Store guidelines.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Every ad request is marked as child-directed (COPPA) and as under the age of consent (TFUA), and limited to general-audience (G) content, so ads are not personalized. The game does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS and does not request the Android advertising ID permission. Google may still process limited technical information (such as IP address, device type and app version) to deliver ads, limit how often an ad is shown and prevent fraud. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy. The No Ads purchase removes ads.

5. Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To fix bugs and tune difficulty, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. Analytics records gameplay events such as a level being started or won; collection is turned off in debug (developer) builds. Crashlytics sends crash and diagnostic reports, such as the error, device model and OS version. This data is not used for advertising.

6. Optional leaderboard

The weekly and monthly leaderboards are optional, and joining is behind a parental gate. If you join, the game uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication and Cloud Firestore (Google) to store a random anonymous user ID, a preset cat name chosen from a list (no free text), your score for the week or month, and your cat's look (fur color and hat). No real names are collected and there is no chat. Boards run per week and per month. To ask for your leaderboard entry to be deleted, email game@dvk.vn.

7. Notifications

The game can send local reminders about the daily puzzle, your streak and free gifts. They are off until the player or a parent turns them on. Reminders are scheduled on the device itself; we do not use a push notification server. No notifications are shown between 21:00 and 9:00. You can turn them off in the game or in your device settings at any time.

8. In-app purchases

Optional purchases (No Ads, 10 hints and 30 hints) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Your store account and its parental controls (Ask to Buy, Family Link) also apply.

9. Your choices and data deletion

You can remove all game data stored on your device by deleting the app. You can leave the leaderboard out entirely by never joining it, and you can ask us to delete a leaderboard entry by emailing game@dvk.vn. Parents can contact game@dvk.vn with any question; we will answer within 30 days.

10. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Contact: game@dvk.vn.
