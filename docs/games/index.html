MASTER THE MECHANICS

How to Play & Score Multiplier Guide

Follow these core tactical principles to climb the global leaderboard and achieve 5,000+ points in every run.

STEP 01

Drag & Place Blocks

Pick one of the 3 available neon blocks from your tray and drag it onto empty cells on the 8×8 grid. Once placed, blocks cannot be moved except with the Undo tool.

STEP 02

Line Blast Clears

Fill an entire horizontal row (8 cells) or vertical column (8 cells) to vaporize all blocks in that line. Cleared space is instantly available for upcoming pieces.

STEP 03

Multi-Line Bonuses

Clear 2, 3, or 4 lines simultaneously in a single placement to earn exponential multi-clear bonus points: 2 lines (+20 pts), 3 lines (+40 pts), 4 lines (+80 pts)!

STEP 04

Consecutive Combo Streaks

Clear at least one line on consecutive turns to build a Combo streak! Multipliers scale from Combo x2 up to x10+ with electrifying English announcer cheers: "Good!", "Great!", "Super Combo!", "Unbelievable!"

STEP 05

The Golden 3×3 Safe Zone

Pro Strategy: Always maintain an open 3×3 square near the center of the board. This prevents sudden large square blocks from causing an unavoidable game over!

STEP 06

100% Offline & Accessible

Zero internet connection required. Features a dedicated ADA Colorblind Mode with distinct geometric symbols on blocks, ensuring comfortable play for everyone.
