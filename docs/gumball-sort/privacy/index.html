Gumball Sort: Ball Sort Puzzle Privacy Policy

How Gumball Sort handles data: no accounts, progress kept on your device, child-directed ads and a parental gate for purchases.

WinPlay Studio · DVK · game@dvk.vn

1. Who we are

Gumball Sort: Ball Sort Puzzle (iOS/iPadOS: com.winplaystudio.sortPuzzle, Android: com.winplaystudio.sort_puzzle) is a puzzle game made by WinPlay Studio (DVK). Questions about this policy: game@dvk.vn. This policy applies to the game; the dvk.vn website has its own privacy page.

2. No accounts, progress stays on your device

The game has no sign-up and no login. Your levels, stars, coins, boosters, candy jars, the level you are playing and your settings (music, sound, color symbols) are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that store player profiles. Deleting the app deletes this data.

3. Made for families, including children

The game is designed to be safe for children. We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, photos, contacts, voice recordings or precise location. There is no chat and no free-text input. Purchases, links to websites and other grown-up actions are behind a parental gate. We follow the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Google Play Families policy and Apple's App Store guidelines.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob (publisher pub-3636940103463538). Every ad request is marked as child-directed and limited to general-audience (G) content, so ads are not personalized and are not based on your interests. The game does not ask for tracking permission on iOS and does not use the Android Advertising ID. Google may still process limited technical information (such as IP address, device type and app version) to deliver ads, limit how often an ad is shown and prevent fraud. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy. Optional reward videos are only shown when the player chooses them. The No Ads purchase turns off banner and between-level ads.

5. In-app purchases

Optional purchases (coin packs, bundles and No Ads) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Your store account and its parental controls (Ask to Buy, Family Link) also apply.

6. Crash reports and anonymous statistics

To fix bugs and balance difficulty, the game may use Firebase Crashlytics, Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Remote Config (Google). These collect technical, non-identifying information such as crash logs, device model, OS version and gameplay events (for example level started, completed or restarted, boosters used). Advertising identifiers are not collected and the data is not used for advertising. Remote Config only adjusts game settings such as how often between-level ads appear.

7. Leaderboards

Leaderboards are optional and use Apple Game Center or Google Play Games. They open only when you tap the trophy button. If you choose to connect, your platform nickname and scores are handled by Apple or Google under their privacy policies. We do not receive your account details.

8. Your choices and data deletion

Because we keep no player accounts, you can remove all game data by deleting the app. You can manage ad privacy choices from the game's Settings where available. Parents can contact game@dvk.vn with any question; we will answer within 30 days.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
