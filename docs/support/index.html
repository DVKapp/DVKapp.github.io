Help us reproduce a problem

Include your app version, device model, operating system, level number and the steps that led to the issue. A screenshot can help. Never send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

About saved progress

Progress is stored on your device. There is no cloud account or server backup in the current version. Please do not uninstall or reset the game while troubleshooting if you want to keep your progress.

Reminders are optional

Enable or disable play reminders in the app’s Settings and in system notification settings. Local reminders are scheduled about 12 and 24 hours after your last play; delivery can be delayed by the operating system.
