Munch Parade: Animal Feast

A picnic feast for a parade of baby animals! Tap a wagon and the animals hop out to munch the matching food on the tray: hippos eat watermelon, koalas eat eucalyptus, flamingos eat shrimp. Clear the whole tray to find the surprise toy hidden inside. Hattie the pygmy hippo leads the parade, explains every feast and cheers you on.

Features

🍉 300 feasts

Every feast is a picture made of real foods: hearts, fish, boats, butterflies, trains and more. Every level is checked by a solver to be winnable.

🦛 Hattie leads the way

A cheerful voice explains the rules, cheers every win and helps when you are stuck with a free Help Paw.

🧸 30 surprise toys

Every feast hides a toy. Fill your Toy Box by playing and with parade tickets.

📖 Who eats what?

Learn real facts about six animals: pygmy hippo, orangutan, chick, koala, flamingo and red squirrel.

🎁 Daily gifts

A free gift every 2 hours and a daily streak bonus. No timers and no lives.

👨‍👩‍👧 Safe for kids

No accounts, no chat, no analytics and no ads. A parental gate guards links and reminders. Works offline.

This version has no ads and no in-app purchases. If that ever changes, we will update our policies first, and any purchase will sit behind a parental gate.
