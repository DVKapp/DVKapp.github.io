Munch Parade: Animal Feast Privacy Policy

How Munch Parade handles data: no accounts, progress kept on your device, no analytics, no ads in this version and a parental gate.

WinPlay Studio · DVK · game@dvk.vn

1. Who we are

Munch Parade: Animal Feast (iOS/iPadOS: com.winplaystudio.munchParade, Android: com.winplaystudio.munch_parade) is a family puzzle game made by WinPlay Studio (DVK) for iPhone, iPad and Android. Questions about this policy: game@dvk.vn. This policy applies to the game; the dvk.vn website has its own privacy page.

2. No accounts, progress stays on your device

The game has no account and no sign-in. Your feasts, stars, surprise toys, parade tickets, the animals you have met, your daily streak, gift timer and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

3. Made for children and families

Munch Parade is designed for children aged 5 and up playing with their families. We do not ask for or collect real names, email addresses, photos, contacts, voice recordings or location, and the game never uses the camera or microphone. There is no leaderboard, no chat, no player-to-player contact and no user-generated content. Hattie the hippo's voice lines are pre-recorded and built into the app. Links to outside websites and turning on reminders are behind a parental gate that asks a grown-up question every time. We follow the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Google Play Families policy and Apple's App Store guidelines.

4. No analytics or crash reports

The game does not include analytics or crash-reporting tools, and we do not collect any analytics or crash data about you.

5. No advertising in this version

This version of Munch Parade shows no ads of any kind and does not use the advertising ID or ask for tracking permission. If a future version offers an optional reward video, we will update this page first. Such videos would always be a choice behind a parental gate, configured as child-directed and non-personalized (Google AdMob, see policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

6. Notifications

The game can send optional reminders (a new feast is ready, keep your streak, your free gift is ready). They are local notifications scheduled on the device itself and are only sent after a grown-up turns them on. No reminders are sent between 9 pm and 9 am. We do not use a push notification server. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in your device settings at any time.

7. No in-app purchases in this version

This version has no in-app purchases. Parade tickets and surprise toys are earned only by playing and cannot be bought or exchanged for money. If purchases are added later, they will be processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, we will never see your payment details, and every purchase will be behind a parental gate.

8. Your choices and data deletion

You can remove all game data by uninstalling the app. Parents can contact game@dvk.vn with any question; we will answer within 30 days.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Contact: game@dvk.vn.
