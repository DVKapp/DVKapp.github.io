Plop: Word Ladder Puzzle

A cheerful word ladder puzzle! Change one letter at a time to climb from the start word to the goal word, and every step must be a real English word. Reach the goal in the fewest steps (par) for 3 stars. Play with Plop, a cute cartoon swirl, on iPhone, iPad and Android.
